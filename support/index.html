Delete your ABBY AI account

You can request deletion of your ABBY AI account and the personal data associated with it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to make this request.

  • From the app: open ABBY AI and request deletion from your account settings.
  • From this page: submit the form below — no app required.
✓

Deletion request received

We'll email the address you provided to verify that you own the account, then permanently delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days.

What happens next

  1. We email the address you provide to verify that you own the account.
  2. Once verified, your account and the personal data associated with it are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  3. We confirm by email when deletion is complete.

What is deleted, and what we may keep

Deleted: your account details (name, email address, phone number), profile information, user-generated content, AI conversation and coaching history, and usage data linked to your account.

May be retained: billing and transaction records, and limited data we are required to keep for security, fraud prevention or legal and regulatory compliance. Any retained data is kept only as long as the law requires. See our Privacy Policy (section 6, Data Retention) for details.

If you subscribed via the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, please also cancel your subscription in your store account — app stores manage subscriptions directly and we cannot cancel them for you.
